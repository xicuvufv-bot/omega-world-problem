## The Winner: Stuttering + Settings Complexity

These two problems are deeply intertwined and affect EVERY PC gamer:
- Stuttering makes games feel broken even on high-end hardware
- Settings tweaking is required to fix stuttering, but the knowledge is fragmented
- No single tool addresses both the symptom (stutter) and the cause (settings/hardware/software)
