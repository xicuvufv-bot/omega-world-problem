# TOP 20 MUSIC FUSIONS
## Best Combinations of Open-Source Components

---

## 1. ACE-Step + Demucs + libsonare
**Best for**: Full song generation with stem control
- ACE-Step generates full song
- Demucs separates into stems
- libsonare mixes and masters
- User can edit individual stems

## 2. MIDI-GPT + FluidSynth + Essentia
**Best for**: MIDI-focused workflow
- MIDI-GPT generates multitrack MIDI
- FluidSynth renders to audio
- Essentia analyzes quality
- Full MIDI editability

## 3. Cadenza + DiffSinger + Keel
**Best for**: Electronic music production
- Cadenza generates MIDI stems (deterministic)
- DiffSinger adds vocals
- Keel automixes and masters
- Seed-based reproducibility

## 4. HeartMuLa + Demucs + DSPark
**Best for**: High-quality full songs
- HeartMuLa generates full song (Suno-quality)
- Demucs separates for editing
- DSPark provides 90+ effects
- Professional result

## 5. Midra + libsonare + damp
**Best for**: Editable, inspectable generation
- Midra generates via agentic pipeline
- libsonare renders and mixes
- damp masters with 6 engine options
- Every step inspectable

## 6. SongGeneration + Demucs + libsonare

- libsonare handles mixing/mastering
- Lowest lyric error rate
