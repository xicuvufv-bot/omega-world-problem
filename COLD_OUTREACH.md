# Cold Outreach Emails — Ready to Send

## How to Find Targets

Search Google for:
- "restaurant near me" → check if they have a website
- "dentist near me" → check their site quality
- "plumber near me" → many have no website at all
- "auto repair near me" → often outdated sites
- "hair salon near me" → usually no website or just Instagram

Look for businesses with:
- No website (just a Google Business listing)
- Old, slow, or broken websites
- Sites that aren't mobile-friendly
- Sites with no contact form or booking

## Where to Send These

1. **Google Maps** — Find local businesses, get their email from their Google listing
2. **Yelp** — Many business listings include email
3. **Instagram** — DM businesses directly (shorter message)
4. **Facebook** — Message their business page
5. **LinkedIn** — Search for business owners in your area

## Tips

- Send 10-20 emails per day
- Personalize each email (mention their actual business name)
- Follow up once after 3 days if no response
- Track responses in a spreadsheet
- Best time to send: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM
