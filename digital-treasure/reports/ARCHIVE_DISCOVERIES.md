## 3. Bountysource Historical Archive

### Timeline:
- **2012**: Founded as open-source bounty platform
- **2013**: Raised $1.1M
- **2017**: Acquired by CanYa (crypto company)
- **2020**: Acquired by The Blockchain Group (French firm)
- **2022**: Terms of service changed to keep unclaimed bounties after 2 years
- **2023**: Community backlash forced reversal
- **June 2023**: Payouts stopped
- **November 2023**: The Blockchain Group bankrupt
- **Losses**: $21,000+ documented, likely much more

### Projects Affected:
- **NewPipe**: ~€6,400 lost
- **FreeCAD**: $4,279 stuck
- **Individual developers**: Unknown total

### Lesson:
- Platform custody = counterparty risk
- Escrow should be decentralized or on-chain

## 5. California AB-1052 Legislative Archive

### Bill Progress:
- **Introduced**: 2025
- **Assembly Vote**: 78-0 (unanimous)
- **Signed**: June 2025
- **Effective**: 2025-2026

### Key Provisions:
- Custodial platforms must report dormant crypto accounts after 3 years
- State holds crypto in native form (not liquidated)
- Owner can reclaim by proving identity
- Applies only to custodial platforms, not self-custody wallets
