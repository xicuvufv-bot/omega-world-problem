## 1. ICO REFUND VAULTS (2017-2019 Era)

### Status: VERIFIED - Smart Contracts Still Active

### What Are RefundVaults?
Many 2017-2019 ICOs used OpenZeppelin's RefundVault or RefundEscrow patterns. These contracts hold contributor funds in escrow until the crowdsale finalizes. If the soft cap was never met, the vault moves to a "Refunding" state - meaning every contributor can still call `refund()` to get their ETH back.

### How to Claim:
1. Visit chainrecoverys.com (BESC Recovery)
2. Connect MetaMask or paste wallet address
3. Scanner automatically detects RefundVault contracts
4. If you contributed to an ICO between 2017-2019 and never received tokens, your ETH may still be claimable
5. Click "Reclaim" - transaction goes directly from contract to your wallet

### Key Details:
- **Cost**: Free tool, only gas fees for claim transaction
- **Time Range**: 2017-2023 ICOs
- **Contracts**: OpenZeppelin RefundVault, RefundEscrow, PostDeliveryCrowdsale
- **Status**: Many contracts dormant for 7-9 years with ETH still claimable
- **Confidence**: VERIFIED - On-chain smart contracts
