## 7. BOUNTY HUNTING STRATEGY

### For Security Researchers:
1. **Start with Web3 bounties** (highest payouts, active programs)
2. **Focus on Immunefi** (largest marketplace, $110M+ paid)
3. **Try Sherlock** for higher signal-to-noise ratio (52% hit rate)
4. **AI bounties** for emerging field (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google)
5. **Traditional bounties** for web/infrastructure skills

### For Developers:
1. **Algora** for GitHub-integrated bounties
2. **OtterSource** for escrow-secure payments
3. **Machdyne** for hardware challenges
4. **Focus on zero-competition bounties** (best ROI)

### Key Metrics to Track:
- **Bounty amount** vs **competition level**
- **Issue status** (open vs closed)
- **Maintainer activity** (last response date)
- **PR review status** (CHANGES_REQUESTED = clear requirements)
- **Payout history** (has the platform paid before?)

### Common Pitfalls:
- **Closed issues with bounties**: 27% of Opire listings
- **Inactive maintainers**: No one to merge your PR
- **Gated bounties**: Invite-only or restricted
- **Platform insolvency**: Bountysource-style failures
- **Scope changes**: Retroactive scope narrowing
