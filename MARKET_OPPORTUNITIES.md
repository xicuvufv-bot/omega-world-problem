## 1. GOVERNMENT PROCUREMENT INTELLIGENCE

### Market Size
- **US Federal Procurement**: $650B+/year
- **State/Local Procurement**: $1.5T+/year
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $2.15T/year
- **Serviceable Market (SMB Intelligence)**: $5B-$10B/year

### Current Players
| Player | Price | Target |
|--------|-------|--------|
| GovWin (D&B) | $10,000-$50,000/year | Enterprise |
| Bloomberg Government | $15,000-$30,000/year | Enterprise |
| Deltek | $5,000-$20,000/year | Enterprise |
| FPDS | Free (raw data) | Government |
| USAspending.gov | Free (raw data) | Researchers |

### Gap
**No affordable intelligence tool for SMBs** ($100-$500/month range)

### Buyer Profile
- 5-50 employee government contractors
- BD managers who manually browse SAM.gov
- Small businesses seeking first federal contract
- Consultants helping clients find government work

### Willingness to Pay
- Current manual process: 2-4 hours/day × $50-$150/hour = $100-$600/day
- Tool saves: 80% of that time = $80-$480/day
- Monthly value: $1,600-$9,600
- Price point: $97-$497/month (fraction of value)
