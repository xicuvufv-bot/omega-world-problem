### Recommendations

1. **Safe to build commercial products on:** audioFlux, noisereduce, Lepton, RobotGo, TagUI, Tantivy
2. **Requires legal review:** OpenFace, OneCut, AudioLDM
3. **Avoid for commercial SaaS:** GPL-licensed projects (unless willing to open source)
4. **Best for commercial use:** MIT-licensed projects (audioFlux, noisereduce, Tantivy)

### Our Winner: audioFlux

**This is the ideal license for a commercial product.**
